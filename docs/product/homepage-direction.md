# ForgeNexus homepage direction

**Decision:** defer implementation until a later pass. The `forge-nexus-ui-concept.html` mock is the direction for ForgeNexus's public homepage.

## Product role

ForgeNexus remains the forum and creator community. Its center of gravity is discussion, discovery, collaboration, live presence, and creator activity.

Sovereign Soul Engine (SSE) is a connected product layer for companion creation, NPC brains, campaign worlds, and group story experiences. SSE should integrate into ForgeNexus later rather than replace the community-first homepage.

## UI direction

Keep the mock's welcoming community homepage structure:

- Community forums as the primary content area
- Discover, Chat, Live, Market, and Members as primary destinations
- Online presence and trending conversations in the side rail
- Clear paths to creator activity and community participation

Future SSE integration can add Companion Forge, Campaign Nexus, Live Rooms, World Feed, and explicit memory/privacy controls.

## Follow-up

When implementation resumes, turn the mock interactions into real flows: forum discovery, conversation opening, replies, follows, live chat, activity feeds, and creator-to-SSE publishing.
